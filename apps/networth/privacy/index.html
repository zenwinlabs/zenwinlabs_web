Privacy Policy

NetWorth Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: July 1, 2026
Published by: ZenWinLabs

1. The short version

NetWorth does not collect any data about you. It cannot. The app has no internet connection, no backend servers, no user accounts, and no way to transmit any information anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The rest of this policy explains this in more detail and covers your data rights.

2. Who we are

NetWorth is an Android application published by ZenWinLabs. You can contact us at:

Email: networth+zenwinlabs@gmail.com
Website: zenwinlabs.com

3. What data NetWorth stores

NetWorth stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Asset names, categories, institutions, and log history (values and dates you enter)
  • Debt names, categories, lenders, and payment history (values and dates you enter)
  • Goal names, target amounts, deadlines, and linked asset configuration
  • App preferences (currency, theme, number format, notification settings)
  • Biometric lock settings (on/off, timeout) — managed entirely by Android's BiometricPrompt API

This data is stored in a local database on your device using Android's Room persistence library. It is not encrypted at the database level by default, but it is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing — no other app can access it.

4. What data NetWorth does NOT collect

NetWorth does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share:

  • Your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Your financial data or any data you enter into the app
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, or similar)
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Any data whatsoever

This is not a policy choice that can be reversed in a future update — it is a consequence of how the app is built. There is no server to send data to.

5. Internet permissions

NetWorth does not request internet permission in its Android manifest. The app is physically incapable of making network requests.

The only external system the app communicates with is the Google Play Store for app delivery and updates — this is handled by the Play Store app on your device, not by NetWorth itself.

6. Third-party SDKs and libraries

NetWorth does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting libraries. The app's dependencies are limited to:

  • Jetpack libraries (Compose, Room, WorkManager, DataStore) — Google's Android development libraries, used locally for app functionality
  • Vico — an open-source charting library for Compose, used locally for graph rendering
  • Inter typeface — served from the app's bundled assets, no external font requests

None of these libraries transmit data externally.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications in Settings, NetWorth uses Android's local notification system (NotificationManager) to send reminders. These are:

  • Monthly balance update reminders
  • Goal milestone alerts (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of a goal reached)
  • EMI due date reminders

All notifications are generated on your device. No notification service, push provider, or external server is involved.

8. Biometric authentication

If you enable app lock, NetWorth uses Android's BiometricPrompt API. This means:

  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint, face) is handled entirely by Android and your device hardware
  • NetWorth never has access to your biometric data
  • NetWorth only receives a pass/fail authentication result from the Android system

9. Data backup and export

NetWorth offers a manual backup and export feature. If you use this:

  • Your data is exported to a file on your device (CSV or JSON format)
  • You choose where to save or share this file (local storage, email, cloud drive)
  • ZenWinLabs has no involvement in, access to, or knowledge of this file or its destination

This is entirely under your control.

10. Google Play

NetWorth is distributed via the Google Play Store. Google may collect certain information as part of the Play Store experience, including installation analytics and crash reports from the Play Store's own systems. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and is separate from NetWorth's own data practices.

11. Children's privacy

NetWorth does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. As the app collects no data at all, there are no special provisions required for users under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

12. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleting the app removes all NetWorth data from your device permanently
  • Using "Clear all data" in Settings removes all app data without uninstalling
  • There is no server-side data to request deletion of

ZenWinLabs holds no copies of your data and has nothing to delete on your behalf.

13. Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, conventional data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) are all exercised directly on your own device. Specifically:

  • Right to access: Your data is in the app. You can view all of it at any time.
  • Right to correction: You can edit or delete any entry in the app at any time.
  • Right to deletion: Delete the app or use "Clear all data" in Settings.
  • Right to portability: Export your full data as CSV or JSON from Settings at any time.

If you have questions about your rights or wish to contact us regardless, reach us at networth+zenwinlabs@gmail.com.

14. Security

NetWorth protects your data through:

  • Android's app sandboxing (no other app can read NetWorth's local database)
  • Optional biometric / PIN lock on the app itself
  • No external transmission of data (nothing to intercept)

We recommend enabling app lock if you share your device with others.

15. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users via an in-app notice on the next app launch.

Given that the app collects no data, material changes to this policy are unlikely.

16. Contact

Email: networth+zenwinlabs@gmail.com

This privacy policy applies to the NetWorth Android application published on the Google Play Store by ZenWinLabs.

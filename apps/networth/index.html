Why NetWorth exists

Most finance apps want access to your bank. We don't.

Linking your bank account to an app means handing your most sensitive data to a company you've never met, running on servers you can't audit, under terms that can change any time.

NetWorth takes a different approach. You enter your numbers. We show you the picture. Nothing leaves your device — not to us, not to anyone.

What you can track

Everything that makes up your financial life.

Pillar 01 · Assets

Every asset you own. Tracked over time.

Log cash, stocks, mutual funds, EPF, PPF, ESOPs, real estate, gold, crypto, FDs, and more. Every time you update a value or make a new investment, NetWorth records it. Over time, you'll see exactly how your wealth has grown — and how much came from your own capital versus the market doing the work.

  • 14 asset categories covering every common investment type
  • Log value updates and new top-ups separately
  • See value vs capital deployed on every asset — know your real return
  • Individual asset graphs with full history
  • Inline sparklines to spot trends at a glance
Pillar 02 · Debt

Track what you owe. Watch it shrink.

Home loans, car loans, credit cards, personal loans — log them all with their outstanding balance and watch the number go down. Set up EMI auto-reduce and NetWorth automatically updates your balance every month without you lifting a finger.

  • Log any type of debt with original amount and current outstanding
  • Set monthly EMI and let the app auto-reduce your balance
  • Track repayment progress with a clear percentage-paid indicator
  • Log additional payments and new disbursements
  • See your projected debt-free date on every loan
Pillar 03 · Goals

Know exactly what it takes to get there.

Set a financial goal, link the assets you're saving in, and NetWorth tells you where you stand. You'll see your actual progress against a projected trajectory — and exactly how much you need to save each month to stay on track.

  • Set any goal with a target amount and deadline
  • Link one or multiple assets to each goal, with partial allocation
  • Define your expected annual growth rate for a realistic projection
  • See actual vs projected trajectory on a clear graph
  • Goals show On Track, Behind, or Achieved status automatically
The big picture

Your entire net worth, at a glance.

The NetWorth dashboard brings it all together. See your total net worth over time, your asset vs debt breakdown, your allocation by category, your goals snapshot, and your recent activity — all on one screen. Switch between 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or all time with a single tap.

  • Live net worth = total assets minus total debt, always up to date
  • Net worth graph across any time period you choose
  • Asset allocation breakdown with category donut chart
  • Snapshot of all your goals with progress rings
  • Activity feed showing every update you've made, grouped by date
  • "This month", "Total gain", and "Debt-free estimate" summary stats
Your data. Full stop.

No cloud. No servers. No strangers reading your numbers.

Every number you enter in NetWorth lives in one place: your phone. We don't have a backend. We don't have user accounts. We have no way to access your data because we've deliberately built the app so that no such access is possible.

This is not a privacy policy promise. It is how the app is built.

No accounts, ever

You don't create an account. There's no username, no password, no email address needed. You open the app and start logging.

No internet required

NetWorth works with your phone in airplane mode. There are no API calls, no syncing, no loading screens. Your data is always there, always instant.

Full data control

Export your data as CSV or as a full JSON backup anytime. Import it back if you reinstall. Your financial history belongs to you.

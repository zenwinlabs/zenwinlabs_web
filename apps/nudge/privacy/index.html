App: Nudge by ZenWinLabs

The short version

Nudge collects no data. Zero. Nothing leaves your phone.

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising SDKs. No account. No email. No name. No location.

Your tasks are yours. They live in your phone's local storage and nowhere else. We have no servers that receive your data because we built no servers for this purpose.

What data does Nudge collect?

None.

Nudge does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data on any external server or third-party service.

The app operates entirely offline. All information you enter — tasks, tags, notes, reminders, patterns — is stored locally on your Android device using Android's Room database. This data never leaves your device.

What permissions does Nudge use?

PermissionWhy it's neededData sent externally?
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo send you task reminders and the evening review promptNo
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMTo fire reminders at the exact time you setNo
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo reschedule your alarms after your phone restartsNo

That's all. Nudge does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage beyond its own app data.

Does Nudge use third-party SDKs or analytics?

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in Nudge.

The app uses the following open-source Android libraries, none of which collect or transmit user data:

  • Room — local database (Google / Apache 2.0)
  • WorkManager — background job scheduling (Google / Apache 2.0)
  • Jetpack Compose — UI framework (Google / Apache 2.0)
  • Vico — chart rendering (Patryk Goworowski / Apache 2.0)
  • Lucide Icons — icon set (Lucide Contributors / ISC)
  • Konfetti — animation library (dionsegijn / MIT)
  • DM Sans & Fraunces — fonts via Google Fonts (Open Font License)

Does Nudge share data with third parties?

No. We have no data to share.

We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user information to any third party, because we do not collect any user information.

Cloud backup and sync

Nudge does not offer cloud sync or cloud backup in its current version.

If you back up your Android device using Google's backup system, Nudge's local data may be included in that device-level backup, subject to Google's own backup and privacy policies. This is controlled entirely by your Android device settings, not by Nudge or ZenWinLabs.

Data deletion

To delete all data created by Nudge:

Option 1 — Inside the app:
Settings → Data → Clear all data. This permanently deletes all tasks, tags, and history.

Option 2 — Android settings:
Android Settings → Apps → Nudge → Storage → Clear Data

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device.

Children's privacy

Nudge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, this applies equally to all users regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes our data practices, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. We will notify users of material changes through an in-app notice.

We do not foresee any reason to collect data. Our design philosophy is offline-first, privacy-by-default.

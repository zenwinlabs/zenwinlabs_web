Nudge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026
App: Nudge by ZenWinLabs
Package: com.zenwinlabs.nudge

The short version

Nudge does not collect your tasks, your name, your email, or your location. Everything you type stays on your phone.

We do use basic anonymous analytics (how many people open the app, which screens are visited) to help us improve Nudge. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do inside Nudge — your tasks, tags, reminders, patterns, and notes — is stored only on your device. It never gets sent to us or anyone else.

We have no account system. We don't know who you are.

What we do collect

We collect anonymous usage data — things like:

  • How many times the app is opened
  • Which screens are visited
  • Crash reports (if the app crashes)

This helps us understand what's working and fix what isn't. None of this data includes your tasks, your name, or anything that could identify you personally.

Permissions the app uses

Nudge asks for a small number of permissions to do its job:

PermissionWhy it's needed
NotificationsSo we can remind you about tasks at the time you set
AlarmsTo make sure reminders fire at exactly the right time, even when your phone is in sleep mode
Run on startupTo restore your reminders if your phone restarts

Nudge does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Do we share your data?

We do not sell your data. Ever.

The anonymous analytics we collect may be processed by our analytics provider, but cannot be linked back to you as an individual.

Deleting your data

To delete everything Nudge has stored on your phone:

Inside the app:
Settings → Data → Clear all data

Or from Android:
Settings → Apps → Nudge → Storage → Clear Data

Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and let you know via an in-app notice.

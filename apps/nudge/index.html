What makes Nudge different

Built for the way fast people actually work

⚡

Type anything. We figure out the rest.

"gym at 7:30pm" adds Gym to today at 7:30 PM. "dentist 23rd july 9am" sets the date and time automatically. No tapping through date pickers. No dropdowns. Just type and go.

🏷

Tags that learn from you.

Type "gym" and Nudge already knows you tag it as Personal. Suggestions get smarter with every task you add — shown as one-tap chips above the keyboard.

📋

Your whole day, at a glance.

Tasks grouped by Morning, Afternoon, and Evening. One pinned Most Important Task at the top. Swipe right to complete. Swipe left to mark missed. Nothing else on screen.

🎯

One task at a time. Nothing else.

When the list feels overwhelming, Focus Mode shows you exactly one task. Tap done, the next one appears. That's it.

🌙

Close your day in 60 seconds.

A fast evening swipe session — done, missed, or carry to tomorrow. Takes under a minute. Tells you exactly where your day went.

📊

See where you keep failing — and why.

Not a dashboard. A mirror. Nudge tells you things like "You miss 70% of tasks scheduled after 8 PM" so you can actually fix the pattern.

Three steps. Every day.

1

Add

2

Your day's tasks grouped by time block. One Most Important Task pinned at the top. Swipe right when done.

3

At 9 PM, a 60-second swipe session closes the day. Done, missed, or carry to tomorrow. Your patterns emerge over time.

Privacy first

Your tasks stay on your phone. Always.

Nudge is 100% private. We built no backend, no server, no database in the cloud. Your tasks, tags, and patterns are stored only in Room (Android's local database) on your device.

We don't collect your name. We don't collect your email. We don't collect your tasks. We collect nothing.

Not because we have to. Because we chose to build it that way.

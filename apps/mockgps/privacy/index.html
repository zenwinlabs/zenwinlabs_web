App: MockGPS by ZenWinLabs

The short version

MockGPS does not collect your real location, your name, your email, or any personal information. The location you choose to mock, and any locations you save, stay entirely on your device.

We do not run a server, and we have no way of seeing where you actually are or where you've told your phone you are.

What we collect

✓ What we collect
  • Nothing. MockGPS does not send any data to us.
✗ What we never collect
  • Your real GPS location
  • Any location you set or save inside the app
  • Your name or email
  • Any information that identifies you personally
  • Payment information

Your data stays on your device

Every saved location, route, and setting inside MockGPS is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in database. We have no access to it. We cannot see it. We cannot delete it remotely.

If you uninstall the app, your data is gone. We don't have a copy — we never had one.

Location permission

MockGPS uses Android's built-in "mock location app" developer setting to provide a simulated location to other apps. This requires the location permission, but that permission is used only to register MockGPS as your device's active location source — never to read, log, or transmit your real location anywhere.

Third parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data. We do not run ads. MockGPS has no analytics or crash-reporting service embedded in it — there is nothing for a third party to receive.

Children's privacy

MockGPS does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, this is straightforward.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Since we collect nothing, we don't expect this to change much.

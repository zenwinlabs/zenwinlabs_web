Three steps. Any location.

1

Enable

Turn on "Select mock location app" in Developer Options and choose MockGPS. One-time setup, about 10 seconds.

2

Set

Drop a pin, search an address, or pick a saved location.

3

Go

Every app on your phone now sees that location, until you switch it off.

Privacy first

Your real location stays on your phone. Always.

MockGPS is 100% offline. We built no backend, no server, no database in the cloud. Your saved locations live only on your device.

We don't collect your name. We don't collect your email. We don't collect your real location or the locations you mock. We collect nothing.

Not because we have to. Because we chose to build it that way.

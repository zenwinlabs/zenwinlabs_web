Trackly Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025
App: Trackly — Know what you pay · Package: com.zenwinlabs.trackly · Developer: ZenWin Labs

The short version

Trackly does not collect your personal data. It does not track you. It does not send your information to any server. Everything you add to Trackly stays on your device. We built this app without needing to know anything about you, and that is intentional.

1. What we collect

Nothing.

Trackly does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information on any server operated by us or any third party.

Specifically, Trackly does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your bank account, credit card, or payment credentials
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP address
  • Any usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Crash reports that include personal or device-identifying information
  • Any financial transaction data

2. Data stored on your device

Trackly stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Subscription names, amounts, billing dates, and notes that you manually enter
  • Reminder preferences you configure
  • App settings (theme, base currency, notification preferences)
  • Price history for subscriptions you edit

This data is stored in an encrypted Room database on your device. It is never transmitted to any server. It is accessible only to the Trackly app.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

Trackly offers an optional backup feature using Google Drive. This feature:

  • Is entirely opt-in. It is not enabled by default.
  • Stores your backup file in the AppData folder of your own Google account. This is a private, app-specific folder that is not visible in your Google Drive file browser and is not accessible to us or any other party.
  • Transmits your subscription data directly from your device to your Google account. The data does not pass through any server we operate.
  • Can be deleted at any time by you, from within the app or from your Google account settings.

When you use Google Drive backup, your data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy in addition to this policy. We do not receive, store, or have access to any data you back up.

4. Google Play Billing

When you purchase Trackly Pro, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made and the product identifier. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information. That data is handled exclusively by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions we request and why

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSRequired to send you renewal reminder notifications. Trackly only sends notifications you have explicitly configured. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARMRequired to deliver reminders at the precise time you set. Used only for notification scheduling on your device. No data leaves your device as a result of this permission.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRequired to reschedule your reminders after your device restarts. Without this, reminders configured before a restart would not fire.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATERequired for Google Drive backup (optional, user-initiated only) and Google Play purchase verification. Trackly does not use internet access for any data collection, analytics, or advertising purposes.

6. Third-party SDKs and libraries

Trackly does not include any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar).

Trackly does not include any advertising SDKs.

Trackly does not include any crash reporting services that transmit personal or device-identifying data.

The only third-party components Trackly uses are:

  • Google Play Billing Library — for processing Pro purchases via Google Play
  • Google Drive API — for the optional user-initiated backup feature
  • Google Sign-In — used only during the Drive backup/restore flow, to authenticate with your Google account

These components are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's privacy

Trackly is a personal finance utility app. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no information from any user, this applies universally.

8. Data retention and deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you are in complete control:

  • To delete all Trackly data: Uninstall the app. All locally stored data is removed from your device automatically.
  • To delete your Drive backup: Open Trackly → Settings → Backup, and delete the backup. Alternatively, revoke Trackly's access from your Google account's app permissions page.
  • To export your data: Use the CSV export feature in Settings (Trackly Pro) to download a copy of your data at any time.

We hold no data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

9. Security

Your subscription data is stored using Android's built-in encrypted database (Room with SQLite encryption). The Google Drive backup file is stored in your private AppData folder, accessible only via your authenticated Google account.

We have no access to your data and therefore cannot be a source of data breach for your personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we ever introduce any data collection (which we do not plan to do), we will notify users within the app before any such change takes effect.

12. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by applicable privacy laws. Since we collect no personal data, the obligations under these laws are satisfied by design.

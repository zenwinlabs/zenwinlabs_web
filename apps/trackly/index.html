What Trackly does

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

💳

Every subscription in one place

Add any recurring payment in under 30 seconds. Choose from 50+ popular services with brand icons, or add your own. Netflix, Spotify, Adobe, gym membership, rent — if it recurs, track it.

🔔

Reminders that actually work

Set a cascade of reminders for each subscription — 7 days before, 3 days, 1 day, on the day, or 1 hour before. Never be blindsided by a charge again. Customise each subscription independently.

Monthly total. Annual total. Category breakdown — how much goes to entertainment vs productivity vs utilities. A 12-month cash flow view that shows exactly when annual renewals stack up. No spreadsheet required.

Add subscriptions in any currency. Set your base currency and Trackly converts everything for your totals. Correct number formatting per locale — ₹1,00,000 for Indian users, $100,000 for US users. Supports 150+ currencies.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your device.

Trackly is built on one rule: we don't need your data to build a great app. There is no account. No login. No server that stores your subscriptions. Everything lives in a database on your phone.

If you choose to back up, your data goes to your own Google Drive account — encrypted, in a folder only Trackly can access. We never see it.

No analytics. No crash reporting that includes personal data. No third-party SDKs that phone home. Just an app that does what it says.

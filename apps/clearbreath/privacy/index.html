ClearBreath Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026 · Last updated: July 2026
Published by: ZenWinLabs · Package: com.zenwinlabs.clearbreath

The short version

ClearBreath does not collect your personal data. Almost everything stays on your device. We do not know who you are, and we have designed the app so we never need to. This policy describes exactly what happens with your data, in plain language.

1. Information we do not collect

ClearBreath does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your location
  • Your device identifier
  • Your IP address
  • Your craving logs
  • Your quit date
  • Your financial data
  • Your voice recordings
  • Any usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Any information about which features you use or how often

We do not use any third-party analytics SDK. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any equivalent service. We do not use any advertising SDK. We do not run A/B tests that transmit data to external servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and DataStore. It never leaves your device except in the specific circumstances described in Section 3.

What ClearBreath stores on your device:

  • Your quit date and time
  • Your daily cigarette count and cost per cigarette, as you entered them during setup
  • Your reason for quitting, as you selected during setup
  • Your craving logs — timestamps, type (resisted or smoked), and triggers you selected
  • Your daily body rating, if you use that feature
  • Your voice check-in audio recordings, if you use that feature
  • Your preferences and settings

This data is used exclusively to show you your own progress within the app. It is processed on your device and never transmitted to ZenWinLabs or any third party.

3. The optional buddy system

ClearBreath includes an optional feature that lets you connect with one other person — a quit buddy. If you choose to use this feature, the following limited data is synced to a Firebase Realtime Database:

  • An anonymised identifier generated from your device — not your name, not your email, not any identifying information
  • Your current streak count
  • Your overall recovery percentage
  • The date you last used the app
  • Short messages you send using the built-in quick reply options

No craving event details are ever synced. Your quit date, cigarette count, cost data, voice recordings, and all other profile information remain on your device only.

You can disconnect from the buddy system at any time in Settings. When you disconnect, your data is deleted from Firebase within 24 hours. If you do not use the buddy system, no data is ever transmitted anywhere.

4. Anonymous community statistics

ClearBreath displays an anonymised community pulse — an aggregate count of how many people are using the app and how many cravings have been collectively resisted. This figure is a server-side aggregate number. Your individual data does not contribute to it in any identifiable way.

If you use the buddy system, your streak and recovery percentage contribute to these aggregate figures in fully anonymised form.

5. Crash reporting

ClearBreath does not use any third-party crash reporting service. If the app crashes, no data is automatically sent anywhere. If you choose to submit a crash report through the Android system prompt, that report goes to Google under Google's privacy policy, not to ZenWinLabs.

6. Google Play and device-level data

ClearBreath is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect information about your use of the Play Store and your device as part of their standard platform operations. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which is separate from this policy and outside our control.

Your Android device may include ClearBreath's local data in an Android backup if you have backup enabled. This backup is stored in your Google account, not accessed by ZenWinLabs.

7. Children

ClearBreath is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Smoking cessation is an adult use case and the app is designed and intended for adult use only.

8. Data security

Because ClearBreath stores data only on your device, the security of that data is governed by your device's security — your lock screen, encryption settings, and physical security. We recommend keeping your device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric.

The optional buddy system data stored in Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest by Firebase's infrastructure.

9. Your rights

Since ClearBreath does not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no data for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. Your data lives on your device and is entirely under your control.

To delete your ClearBreath data: open Settings in the app and select Reset app data. This permanently deletes all locally stored data. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.

For buddy system data: disconnect from the buddy system in Settings. Your data is removed from Firebase within 24 hours.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this document and make the updated policy available at the same URL. For significant changes, we will post a notice within the app.

12. Open source acknowledgements

ClearBreath is built using open source libraries including Jetpack Compose, Room, Hilt, Lottie for Android, and Rive. These libraries are used under their respective open source licences. Their use does not affect your privacy in any way described in this policy.

This privacy policy applies to the ClearBreath Android application published on the Google Play Store by ZenWinLabs.

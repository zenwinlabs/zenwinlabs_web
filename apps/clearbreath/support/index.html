Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. ClearBreath works without an account, an email address, or any personal information. Open the app, complete the 2-minute setup, and start.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Your craving logs, your quit date, your voice recordings, your progress — all of it lives locally on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers. See the Privacy Policy for the complete details.

I slipped and smoked. Should I reset my quit date?

No. One slip does not restart your quit. Log it honestly in the app using the I Smoked button, note what triggered it, and continue. ClearBreath does not penalise slips — it tracks them as data points in your pattern. Your quit date remains the date you set it.

The anatomy visualization looks different than it did last week. Is that correct?

Yes. The anatomy visualization updates continuously based on your days smoke-free. Your lungs change appearance as you pass recovery thresholds. If you are on day 14, your lungs should look meaningfully different from day 1. That change is intentional and medically grounded.

How does the craving forecast work?

After 7 days of logged craving events, ClearBreath analyses your personal pattern across 48 thirty-minute time windows throughout the day. It identifies your three historically highest-risk windows and schedules a notification 5 minutes before each one. The forecast updates every week as new data comes in.

What is the buddy system?

The buddy system lets you connect with one other person — a quit partner or someone supporting you. You can see each other's streak and recovery percentage, and exchange short quick-reply messages. It requires both people to have ClearBreath installed. You connect using a 6-character invite code generated in Settings.

Can I change my quit date?

Yes. Go to Settings and select Quit details. You can edit your quit date there. Changing it will recalculate all your stats from the new date.

Will ClearBreath remind me to use it?

ClearBreath has a range of smart notifications that fire based on your pattern and progress — milestone alerts, streak protection, craving prediction, and a weekly report. You can enable or disable each notification type individually in Settings.

What is Emergency Mode?

Emergency Mode is a full-screen 5-minute craving intervention launched from the floating button on the Home screen. It is designed for moments when a craving is overwhelming enough that the regular Home screen tools are not enough. It runs a breathing animation, a live countdown, and rotating personalised messages — and connects directly to the Falling Block game if you need more help.

I have feedback or found a bug.

We want to hear it. Email clearbreath+zenwinlabs@gmail.com with the subject line "ClearBreath feedback" or "ClearBreath bug." Include your Android version and a description of what happened. We read every message.

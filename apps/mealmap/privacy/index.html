MealMap Privacy Policy

ZenWinLabs · Last updated: July 2026

The short version

MealMap collects no personal data. Nothing you enter into the app — your meals, recipes, macro goals, grocery lists, or health preferences — ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing.

MealMap has no backend server. No database in the cloud. No analytics SDK. No crash reporter that phones home. No advertising network.

We do not collect:

  • Your name or email address
  • Your meal plans or recipes
  • Your calorie or macro data
  • Your dietary preferences
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers
  • Your usage patterns or behaviour inside the app

Where your data lives

Everything you create in MealMap — recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, reminders, preferences — is stored exclusively in Room, Android's on-device local database, on your phone or tablet.

If you uninstall MealMap, that data is removed along with the app. We have no copy of it. No one does.

Recipe images

When you add a meal without a photo, MealMap can optionally fetch a relevant food image from Unsplash (unsplash.com) using their public API. This request goes from your device directly to Unsplash's servers. We do not see, log, or store any information about this request.

If your device is offline, no request is made. The image is downloaded once and stored locally on your device for all future uses.

Unsplash's own privacy policy governs that interaction: unsplash.com/privacy

Notifications and reminders

Meal reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Android's AlarmManager. No reminder data, timing, or meal information is sent anywhere. Reminders fire entirely on-device.

Permissions we request

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo show meal reminders you configure
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMTo fire reminders at the exact time you set
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo restore your reminders after a device restart
INTERNETOnly used to fetch Unsplash images when you add a meal without a photo
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo check connectivity before attempting an Unsplash image fetch

We request only what the app needs to work. Nothing more.

Third-party services

MealMap uses one external service:

Unsplash (unsplash.com)
Used to fetch food photography for meals that don't have a user-provided image. Your device makes a direct API request to Unsplash. We do not act as an intermediary and do not log these requests. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to their handling of the request.

That is the complete list.

Children

MealMap does not target children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how MealMap handles data, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Given that we collect nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely.

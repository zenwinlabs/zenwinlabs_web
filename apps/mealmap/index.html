What makes MealMap different

Built for people who actually meal prep

🗺

Your week, mapped out.

Add meals to any day. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks. Your calorie and macro totals update as you plan. See exactly what you're eating before you eat it.

🤖

Add any recipe in seconds with AI.

Find a recipe anywhere — Instagram, YouTube, a blog. Paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini with our one-tap prompt. Paste the result back. MealMap fills in the title, ingredients, macros, steps, and storage instructions automatically.

📊

Know your numbers.

Every meal shows calories, protein, carbs, and fat. Tap any segment of the macro ring to see how it fits your daily goal. Adjust servings and everything updates instantly.

🛒

A grocery list that actually makes sense.

MealMap builds your shopping list from your weekly plan. Fresh produce and pantry staples are grouped separately. Mark what you already have. Scale for 2 days or 30 — quantities adjust automatically.

🍽

Scale any recipe to any serving.

Every recipe stores quantities for a base serving count. Cooking for one today, four on Sunday? Slide the serving count and every ingredient scales with it.

📅

Copy your best days.

Had a great nutrition day on Wednesday? Long press it and copy the entire meal plan to any other day of the month. Replace or merge — your choice.

🌿

Personalised to how you eat.

Vegetarian, eggetarian, or non-vegetarian — MealMap filters recipes to match. Select the proteins you actually eat and we skip the rest. Your explore feed shows only what's relevant to you.

🔔

Reminders that keep you on track.

Set a reminder for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snacks independently. MealMap nudges you at the right time so meals don't slip past you on busy days.

Three things. Every week.

1

Plan

Add your meals for the week. Browse curated recipes or import any recipe from the internet using AI. Adjust servings, check your macros, and see your daily totals come together.

2

Prep

MealMap builds your shopping list from your plan. Grouped by fresh and pantry items. Mark what you already own. Scale for however many days you're shopping for.

3

Track

Open Insights to see how your week went — daily, weekly, and monthly. Tap any day to see exactly what you ate and how it tracked against your goals.

Privacy first

Your meals stay on your phone. Always.

MealMap is 100% offline. We built no backend, no server, no database in the cloud. Your recipes, your meal plans, your grocery lists, and your nutrition data are stored only in Room — Android's local database — on your device.

We don't collect your name.

We don't collect your meals.

We don't collect your health data.

We collect nothing.

Not because we have to. Because we chose to build it that way.

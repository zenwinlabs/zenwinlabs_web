FastWell Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
Published by: ZenWinLabs · Contact: fastwell+zenwinlabs@gmail.com

The short version

FastWell is an intermittent fasting tracker built on a single principle: your health data belongs to you and no one else. FastWell stores your data locally on your device, does not send it anywhere, and has no way to access it.

1. Who we are

FastWell is developed and published by ZenWinLabs. We are reachable at fastwell+zenwinlabs@gmail.com for any questions about this policy.

2. What data FastWell collects

FastWell collects information that you voluntarily enter into the app. This includes:

Personal information entered during setup:

  • First name (optional)
  • Age
  • Biological sex (optional, used for metabolic calculations)
  • Height
  • Current weight and goal weight

Health and wellness data logged during use:

  • Fasting sessions — start time, end time, duration, plan used
  • Mood after fasts — energy level, hunger level, focus level, notes
  • Water intake — amount, drink type, time
  • Body weight — value, date, notes
  • Body measurements — waist, chest, hips, arms, thigh, date

App usage data:

  • Active fasting plan
  • App preferences and settings
  • Achievement and XP progress

3. Where your data is stored

All data collected by FastWell is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database (Room). Your data is not transmitted to any server operated by ZenWinLabs or any third party. Your data does not leave your device unless you explicitly choose to create a Google Drive backup (see Section 5).

4. Data we do not collect

FastWell does not collect, transmit or store any of the following:

  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • IP address or location data
  • Crash analytics or performance metrics sent to external servers
  • Any data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Any data shared with third parties for any purpose

We have no server-side database. We have no user accounts. We have no way to identify you or access your data. This is by design.

5. Google Drive backup (optional)

FastWell offers an optional backup feature that exports your data to your personal Google Drive account. If you choose to use this feature:

  • FastWell requests access to a private app folder in your Google Drive account (Drive AppData scope)
  • This folder is only accessible by FastWell and by you — it is not visible to other apps or to us
  • Your data is stored in your Google Drive account, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • You can delete this backup at any time from your Google Drive settings
  • If you do not use the backup feature, no data is sent to Google Drive

FastWell does not have access to the rest of your Google Drive. The backup scope is limited to a private app folder only.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, FastWell uses Android's notification system to send local push notifications for:

  • Fasting stage milestones
  • Fast completion
  • Eating window reminders
  • Scheduled fast reminders

These notifications are generated locally on your device. No data is sent to any server to generate or deliver them. You can disable notifications at any time from the app settings or your device notification settings.

7. Permissions

FastWell may request the following Android permissions:

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo send fasting stage and reminder notifications
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMTo trigger precise fasting start/end notifications
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo reschedule alarms after device restart

FastWell does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other sensitive permissions.

8. Children's privacy

FastWell is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has entered data into the app, please contact us and we will assist in removing it from the device.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data exists only on your device. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual entries from within the app (fasting history, weight logs, water logs, measurements)
  • Clear all data from Settings → Data → Clear all data
  • Uninstall the app — this permanently deletes all locally stored data

If you used Google Drive backup, your backup remains in your Google Drive until you delete it manually from your Drive account.

10. Data security

FastWell uses Android's built-in application sandbox, which prevents other apps from accessing your data. The Room database used by FastWell benefits from Android's file-level encryption on supported devices (Android 6.0 and above with device encryption enabled).

We recommend keeping your device encrypted and secured with a PIN or biometric lock as a general security practice.

11. Third-party services

FastWell does not integrate any third-party analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel or similar). FastWell does not display advertisements and does not use any advertising SDKs.

The only optional third-party integration is Google Drive, which is used solely for the user-initiated backup feature described in Section 5.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify users through the app on the next launch. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Your rights

Because your data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, the typical data rights requests (access, correction, deletion, portability) are managed entirely by you through the app itself and your device settings. We have nothing to provide, correct or delete on our end.

If you have questions about your rights or this policy, contact us at fastwell+zenwinlabs@gmail.com.

Why FastWell exists

Most fasting apps want an account before they'll let you track a fast.

DoFasting. Zero. BodyFast. They ask you to log in, hand over an email address, and trust them with deeply personal health data — your weight, your body, your daily patterns.

FastWell does the same thing — and more — without a login, an account, or access to your personal health data.

Your fasting data belongs to you. We built FastWell that way on purpose.

What FastWell does

Everything you need to fast well.

Built around the three things that actually move the needle — your fast, your hydration, your weight.

⏱

A timer that knows what's happening inside you.

FastWell doesn't just count hours. It tracks your metabolic stage in real time — from glycogen burning to fat burning to ketosis to autophagy — and shows you exactly what your body is doing and why it matters.

  • 6 metabolic stages, live tracking
  • Visual body silhouette that lights up
  • Streaks, history and mood logging
📋

Pick your plan. Change it anytime.

Every major intermittent fasting protocol, from the gentle 12:12 for beginners to OMAD for experienced fasters. We suggest a plan during setup — switch anytime, even mid-fast.

  • 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD
  • Fully custom windows and days
💧

Hydration is half the battle during a fast.

Log water and other drinks throughout the day. Watch your animated water drop fill up. Set a custom daily goal, choose your cup size, and log coffee, tea, juice or anything else that counts.

⚖️

Watch the number move. Understand why it moves.

Log your weight daily and watch your trend line take shape. Set a goal weight and timeline during setup — FastWell shows a live projection so you know exactly what to expect.

📏

Track what the scale doesn't show.

Waist, chest, hips, arms and thigh measurements — the numbers that show real body composition change even when the scale isn't moving. Sparklines show the trend at a glance.

📊

Your data. Your patterns. Clearly.

The Report tab brings together your fasting, hydration and mood data into one view. See your average fasting duration against your goal, which days you struggled, and your mood patterns across fasts.

🏅

Every fast is worth celebrating.

A full XP and badge system that rewards consistency. Complete fasts, hit streaks, reach milestones — earn badges and level up from Spark all the way to Eternal Flame.

  • 15 badges, 8 levels
  • Streaks and level-up celebrations
📖

Understand what you're doing and why it works.

A built-in knowledge base covering every aspect of intermittent fasting — the science, the first week, what to drink, common problems, nutrition and more. Bundled in the app, no internet required.

🏠

Glance at your fast without opening the app.

FastWell's home screen widget shows your elapsed time, current metabolic stage and fast progress at a glance. Available in two sizes. Fully private, nothing leaves your phone.

🔐

Your health data is yours. We can't see it. We don't want to.

Everything is stored locally in an encrypted database. There is no server, no account, and no way for us to access your data — because we deliberately built it so we can't.

Privacy first

Built without a backend on purpose.

We didn't skip building a server because it was hard. We skipped it because your intermittent fasting data — your weight, your body measurements, your daily routine — is exactly the kind of personal health information that shouldn't sit on someone else's servers.

FastWell is entirely local. No sync. No account. No profile. No analytics tracking your health habits. Just an app that works, entirely on your phone. If you want a backup, FastWell supports optional Google Drive backup to your own private Drive folder — never ours.
